# PRODUCTION BRIEF — UPTIME CINEMATIC LAUNCH FILM

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
- **Product**: Uptime (PC Uptime)
- **Platform**: Windows 10 / Windows 11 Desktop (WPF & Avalonia)
- **Website**: https://uptime-pc.vercel.app/
- **Store**: Microsoft Store (ID: `9p7h0nn62hk0`)
- **Category**: System Monitoring, Stability Forensics & Power Operations
- **Target Audience**: Power users, developers, sysadmins, hardware enthusiasts, PC builders, and professionals who demand absolute reliability, zero telemetry, and quiet precision.

## 2. CORE VALUE PROPOSITION & MESSAGING
- **The Quiet Observer**: Runs silently in the background, respecting system resources with zero telemetry and 100% local event analysis.
- **Precision Metrics**: Accurate boot tracking, session duration, active vs. idle ratio, and kernel thread health.
- **Stability Forensics**: 30-day stability scoring, crash detection, unexpected shutdown analysis, and Windows event recovery metrics.
- **Power Operations**: Fast Startup bypass, clean restart, timed shutdown countdown, and direct-to-UEFI reboot.
- **Instant Access**: Compact translucent Mini-HUD widget with global shortcut (`Win` + `Alt` + `U`) and native system tray presence.

## 3. CREATIVE GOALS & TONE
- **Visual Tone**: Inspired by Apple product films and Braun/Dieter Rams industrial minimalism — restrained obsidian surfaces (`#0F1114`), slate cards (`#1B1E24`), crisp typography, subtle metallic rim lighting, and a signature emerald sage glow (`#7BC4A8`).
- **Pacing**: Deliberate, confident, architectural. Begins contemplative and mechanical, builds progressive momentum through precision motion choreography, and peaks with an authoritative logo lockup.
- **Sound Direction**: Custom cinematic electronic soundscape: deep sub-bass drones (30–60 Hz), tactile mechanical micro-clicks, resonant filter sweeps, rhythmic riser pulses, and a triumphant yet understated closing chord.
- **Prohibitions**: No generic AI tropes, no neon cyberpunk clichés, no artificial particles, no fake 3D wireframe heads, no misleading stats.

## 4. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- **Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 Full HD)
- **Frame Rate**: 60.0 FPS
- **Duration**: 40.0 Seconds (2400 Frames)
- **Color Space**: Rec.709 / sRGB
- **Engine**: Remotion 4.x + React + Canvas / SVG + Custom Procedural Audio
- **Audio Output**: 48 kHz, 24-bit Stereo Master
- **Video Codec**: H.264 (High Profile, Level 4.2), BT.709, YUV420p
